**Friends of Tribals Society**

## Live Form
🌐 **[Open CPR Form](https://ftscpr.github.io/fts-cpr-system/)**

## About
Digital version of the FTS Chapter Performance Report (CPR) format.

### Sections Covered:
- **Header** – Chapter Name, Month, CPP Name, Submission Date
- **A | Membership Data** – Chapter Members, Mahila Samiti, Yuva Samiti
- **B | Executive Committee Meeting Details**
- **C | Donation Details** – OTS Details, OTS Collection, Donor Detail
- **D | CSR Details**
- **E | Seva Patra (Collection Boxes)**
- **F | Investment (As on Date)**
- **G | Van Yatra**
- **Events During the Month** (with photo uploads)
- **Annexure 1** – Disbursement to Anchal
- **Annexure 2** – Monthly Summary / Check Points
